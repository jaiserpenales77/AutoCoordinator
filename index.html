            Account Types

            • PLT — Unplanned Issue, Bulk Return and the Bulk Calculator. Everyone who signs up starts here.
            • MLT — every form except the Hold Tag, and the Bulk Calculator.
            • Lead — everything except this tab: yield sheets, Stored Data and all forms. Leads are in the Created By list.
            • Admin — everything, and this tab: change account types and the leads list.
            • Owner — an admin who can't be demoted or removed here; only owners can demote admins. Owners are the usernames in the owners list of the pk030/admins document, changed in the Firebase console.

            PLT and MLT never reach the saved yield sheets or Stored Data.

            Activity Log

            What everyone does in the app, and every sign-in to LineUp, Cremer Recipes and this app with its IP address, grouped by name (A–Z), newest first for each. LineUp and Cremer Recipes sign in with shared accounts, so they show the shift or login, not a person. Each account lists the networks it signed in from; an Owner can press Allow to add one to LineUp's allowed networks, until removed or for 30 days. Entries can't be changed or deleted from the app.

            Rotation Schedule

            Type in the blanks and click a box to mark it; print it blank to fill in by hand. For Auto rotation, type the positions (left column) and the 1st rotation's names, then fill in the rest from the panel.

            Auto rotation

            Skill-based: people only go on positions they're trained on (set in Skills, under the sheet), move off their last position each rotation, and share the positions and time off evenly.

            Stays put (doesn't rotate)

              Skills: who's trained on what

              Everyone starts trained on every position. Untick a box when someone isn't trained on that position, and Auto rotation won't put them there. Remembered on this computer, by name and position.

              Type the positions and the 1st rotation's names on the sheet (and any extra people), and the grid appears here.

              Component Transfer

              Component Transfer Notice: what component (and its item number) and how much moved, from and to which work order and line, why, and the lead's and supervisor's names. Long entries shrink to fit; it prints on one page.

              Training Sign-in Sheet

              TR002B [CA] Training Sign-in Sheet. Department is filled in with PACKAGING; print it like that for people to fill in by hand, or type in any boxes first. Click a Delivery Method to circle it and a Training Type to tick it; click again to clear. Page 2 has room for 31 more names and picks up page 1's Course # and Date.

              Trouble Shooting Template

              Start this form when there's an equipment failure. Print it blank to fill in by hand, or type in any boxes first; an empty Date and Time start with today's date and the time now. Click the circle beside a row to circle that check as the cause (click again to clear).

              Prepare for Your Pulse Check Conversation

              Print it blank to jot on by hand, or type your answers on the lines first; longer answers carry on down the box. Nothing typed here is saved or sent anywhere: it's gone when you leave the page or click Clear.

              HOLD TAG

              QA263C [ALL] QA Hold Tag. Type in the boxes; Work Order/Lot #, Tote/Pallet # and Reason/Comment take several lines. It prints like the paper tag, red page included; turn off Red background to print on red paper.

              Standard Work

              Team Lead Standard Work (front) and Steady State Tasks & Habits (back). Type in the blanks and boxes, and click a box to tick it: once for a tick, twice for N/A, three times to empty it. It prints both sides, front then back, for double-sided printing.
