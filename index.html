      Account Types

      • PLT — Unplanned Issue, Bulk Return and the Bulk Calculator. Everyone who signs up starts here.
      • MLT — every form except the Hold Tag, and the Bulk Calculator.
      • Lead — everything except this tab: yield sheets, Stored Data and all forms. Leads are in the Created By list.
      • Admin — everything, and this tab: change account types and the leads list.
      • Owner — an admin who can't be demoted or removed here; only owners can demote admins. Owners are the usernames in the owners list of the pk030/admins document, changed in the Firebase console.

      PLT and MLT never reach the saved yield sheets or Stored Data.

      Forms

      Pick a form to fill in and print. Each form keeps what you typed while you switch between forms and tabs.

      Unplanned Issue

      [VALA] Unplanned Issue Request Form (PK007B). Type in the blanks and table, and click a line next to the issue type to mark it with an X; it prints on one page like the paper form.

      Bulk Return

      PK120A [VALA] Packaging - Bulk Return (QS106A). Type in the blanks and click a reason to circle it; it prints exactly like the paper form.

      HOLD TAG

      QA263C [ALL] QA Hold Tag. Type in the boxes; Work Order/Lot #, Tote/Pallet # and Reason/Comment take several lines. It prints like the paper tag, red page included; turn off Red background to print on red paper.

      Standard Work

      Team Lead Standard Work (front) and Steady State Tasks & Habits (back). Type in the blanks and boxes, and click a box to tick it: once for a tick, twice for N/A, three times to empty it. It prints both sides, front then back, for double-sided printing.
